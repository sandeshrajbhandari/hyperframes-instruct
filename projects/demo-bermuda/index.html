The Bermuda Triangle is one of the world's most famous mysteries.
Located between Florida, Bermuda, and Puerto Rico, it has been linked to stories of ships and planes mysteriously disappearing.
One of the most famous cases happened in 1945, when five US Navy aircraft vanished during a training mission.
But is the area actually dangerous?
Scientists have found no evidence that disappearances happen here more often than in other busy parts of the ocean.
Strong storms, unpredictable weather, and powerful currents may explain many of the incidents.
So, there may be no supernatural mystery after all. Just a fascinating legend that refuses to disappear.
